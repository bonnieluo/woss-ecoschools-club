Who We Are & What We Believe

Our Mission

Empowering students to drive lasting environmental change at White Oaks and beyond.

Founded by Bonnie, with Bonnie and Elif serving as Co-Presidents, the WOSS EcoClub champions environmental stewardship across White Oaks Secondary School in Oakville, Ontario. We bridge the gap between climate awareness and hands-on school community action—turning sustainability from an abstract idea into tangible projects that enhance biodiversity, cut energy waste, and cultivate youth leadership.

Waste Reduction & Circular Initiatives

Diverting single-use plastics from landfills through school supply swaps, classroom sorting drives, and circular student-sharing programs that make reuse effortless.

StudyCycle · Locker Cleanouts · Zero-Waste

Campus Biodiversity & Green Spaces

Restoring ecological balance in our courtyard and school perimeter by planting drought-tolerant native perennials, maintaining pollinator stations, and nurturing tree canopies.

Pollinator Garden · Native Flora · Habitat Care

Climate Education & Community Action

Organizing student-led workshops, monitoring classroom energy efficiency, and hosting community cleanups to inspire daily sustainable habits across the entire student body.

Club Initiatives & Projects

Explore our ongoing programs, celebrate past milestones, and preview upcoming campaigns designed to make White Oaks greener.

StudyCycle

Active Project

A circular student-sharing initiative where students can donate, swap, and reuse gently used school supplies and textbooks to reduce campus waste.

Focus: Circular Economy & Student Equity
Location: WOSS Main Foyer & Library Hub
Schedule: Open Daily for Drop-Off & Exchange
StudyCycle provides collection bins in the main foyer before midterms and at year-end locker cleanouts. Donated binders, calculators, and textbooks are inspected, sorted, and redistributed for free at the start of each semester, saving students money and keeping hundreds of kilograms of usable supplies out of landfills.

Native Pollinator Garden

Active Project

A hands-on restoration project on school grounds where members plant and maintain native flora to support local biodiversity and ecosystems.

Focus: Biodiversity & Urban Habitat Restoration
Flora: Coneflowers, Milkweed, Wild Bergamot
Workdays: Bi-weekly Gardening After School
Constructed in partnership with local ecological nurseries, the garden features drought-resistant southern Ontario perennials that require zero artificial fertilizers or heavy watering. Student caretakers mulch, weed, track pollinator visits, and cultivate natural habitat for native bees and monarch butterflies.

Seasonal Oakville Cleanups

Completed Milestone

Seasonal outdoor events where student volunteers gather to collect litter and keep our school grounds and local Oakville parks clean.

Impact: 180+ kg of Litter & Plastic Diverted
Turnout: 85+ High School Volunteers
Partners: Town of Oakville Park Stewards
Our spring and autumn cleanups mobilized student teams along McCraney Valley Park and White Oaks ravines. All recovered materials were sorted into recyclable metals, hard plastics, and landfill waste, with hazardous items handled safely by municipal coordinators.

School-Wide Waste & Energy Audits

Completed Milestone

A monitoring program to audit classroom recycling and run energy conservation challenges across WOSS.

Reach: 65 Classrooms & Science Labs Audited
Result: 25% Reduction in Phantom Lighting
Recognition: HDSB Green School Excellence Ribbon
Student audit squads logged contamination rates in classroom paper and blue bins while verifying that computer monitors and projector units were powered down after final period. Classes received eco-rating stickers and teachers competed in our "Lights-Out Lunch" challenge.

Climate Awareness Days

Upcoming Campaign

Interactive campaigns designed to educate the student body on sustainable daily habits and youth climate action.

Target Date: Spring Earth Week & World Environment Day
Highlights: Guest Youth Speakers & Trivia Booths
Student Role: Campaign Booth Organizers & Speakers
Upcoming activities include a school-wide eco-pledge banner signing, interactive lunchtime carbon footprint quizzes, and a zero-waste cafeteria showcase featuring sustainable lunch packing tips and local climate science panels.
